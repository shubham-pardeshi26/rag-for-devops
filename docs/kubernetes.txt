Kubernetes is a container orchestration platform that automates deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.

Common Kubernetes errors and solutions:

Error: CrashLoopBackOff

Fix: Check pod logs using kubectl logs <pod-name> and inspect for application failures.

Error: ImagePullBackOff

Fix: Verify the image exists and the credentials are correct.

Error: Node Not Ready

Fix: Check node status with kubectl get nodes and ensure kubelet is running.

Error: Service Not Accessible

Fix: Ensure service type and port configurations are correct.

Error: Persistent Volume Claim Pending

Fix: Verify that storage classes and volume availability match the request.
